# Changelog

## [1.0.0] — 2026-10-23

First public release.

- Full-window Fuji at dusk / Lawson photo background.
- Glass shell with adjustable brightness, saturation, zoom, mist and grain.
- Nine material finishes: cold mist, fluted, smoked, frosted, iridescent,
  brushed metal, paper, film, rain.
- Four edge modes: soft, chrome, neon, bevelled.
- Light-leak colour and strength, vignette, surface sheen, texture scale.
- Composer, settings panel and quote bar share the same material language.
- 1,236 quotes across emotion / motivation / football / mixed.
- Two-minute quote auto-rotation with manual refresh.
- System-time clock, searchable world locations, Open-Meteo weather.
- Cinematic startup animation with replay and skip.
- All settings persist in local storage.
